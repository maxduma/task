DejaVu Sans (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf)

These fonts are used to render Cyrillic and Latin-diacritic text in generated charts and PDFs.

DejaVu fonts are based on the Bitstream Vera Fonts and the Arev fonts and are distributed under
the permissive DejaVu Fonts license, which allows use, redistribution, and modification (including
bundling with software) provided the copyright and license notices are retained. The fonts are not
